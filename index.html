FAQ

Questions founders ask first

What is the difference between an agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent pursues a goal: it plans steps, uses tools such as your CRM or email, checks results, and asks a human when it is unsure. That is what lets it finish a task, not just discuss it.

Which AI models do you use?

We choose per task. That usually means a frontier model such as Claude, OpenAI or Gemini for reasoning, and smaller models where cost and speed matter. We design so models can be swapped without rebuilding the system.

Is my business data safe?

We use least-privilege access, keep secrets out of code, and can restrict what leaves your environment. We will review data sensitivity with you before building, and can use providers with no-training data terms.

What if the agent makes a mistake?

Agents are not perfect, so we design for that: approval gates on risky actions, confidence thresholds, test sets, logging, and easy rollback. The aim is measured, bounded risk.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. You own the code, prompts and configuration delivered. Ongoing model and hosting costs are billed by the providers directly to you where possible.

Do you work with small businesses and individual founders?

Yes. Pilots are scoped to be affordable, so a single agent can prove value before any larger commitment.
